### 🧩 Problem

SaaS providers struggle to build meaningful relationships with customers due to lack of effective tools for customer discovery, research, communication, and relationship management. Existing solutions are complex and not built specifically for SaaS.

### 🚀 Solution

All-in-one platform with Google Analytics-like installation, customer base browsing, individual customer research, in-app messaging, and relationship management metrics. Enables contextual communication and customer tracking.

### 📈 Traction

Product in development since January, ready for public beta. Private beta testers showing strong positive feedback.

### 👥 Team

Eoghan McCabe (CEO), Des Traynor (COO), Ciaran Lee (CTO), David Barrett (Frontend). Team previously ran Contrast consultancy and launched successful startups Qwitter and Exceptional (both acquired).

### 🌍 Market

Initial focus on SaaS providers ($21B market, projected $93B by 2016 per Forrester). Future expansion to mobile and desktop app providers.

### 🧱 Moat

Integrated all-in-one solution specifically designed for SaaS, combining features that currently require multiple separate tools.

### ⚠️ Risks

Crowded competitive landscape across multiple categories (social media research, customer support, email campaigns, analytics). Requires significant adoption for network effects.
