### 🧩 Problem

Students need a way to connect with others at their school and across universities through social networks and shared interests

### 🚀 Solution

Online directory platform with user profiles, social networking features, course integration, and messaging system connecting college communities

### 📈 Traction

70,000 total users, 65% daily active users, 95% monthly active users, 90 million monthly pageviews as of March 2004. Expanded to 16+ universities in 2 months

### 👥 Team

Eduardo Saverin (CFO)

### 🌍 Market

15 million college students in US with $85 billion purchasing power. Students spend across restaurants, automobiles, clothes, phones and other amenities

### 🧱 Moat

First-mover in college social networking, verified .edu network effect, course integration database

### ⚠️ Risks

Rapid expansion execution, competition from other social networks, reliance on college market
