### 🧩 Problem

Taxi industry plagued by inefficient technology, poor service quality, and expensive medallion system. Drivers underpaid, no accountability, and significant dead-time in fare seeking.

### 🚀 Solution

Members-only on-demand car service using mobile apps and GPS technology. Offers luxury vehicles, professional drivers, and automated dispatch for guaranteed pickups.

### 📈 Traction

5 advisors and 15 clients recruited. Website, trademark, and provisional patent filed. Ready for 3-car pilot and app development.

### 🌍 Market

$4.2B annual market in US with top 4 players only controlling 22%. Initial focus on SF/NYC, expanding to LA, Chicago, Houston, PA, Dallas covering 50% of US market.

### 🧱 Moat

Patent-pending system design. Members-only model with reputation tracking. Automated dispatch and route optimization technology. Digital hail approach bypasses medallion requirements.

### ⚠️ Risks

Regulatory challenges with taxi medallion system. Capital intensive fleet requirements. Competitive response from established players. Early stage execution risks.
